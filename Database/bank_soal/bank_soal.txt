## S5 1
Q: She ____ milk every morning.
+ drinks
- drink
- drinking
- to drink
E: Subjek "she" memakai kata kerja + s/es: drink -> drinks.

Q: I ____ my hands before eating.
+ wash
- washes
- washing
- to wash
E: Subjek "I" memakai kata kerja bentuk dasar tanpa -s: wash.

Q: My father ____ to the office by car.
+ goes
- go
- going
- to go
E: "My father" sama dengan "he", jadi go -> goes (kata berakhiran -o ditambah -es).

Q: They ____ football every Sunday.
+ play
- plays
- playing
- to play
E: Subjek "they" memakai kata kerja bentuk dasar: play.

Q: The cat ____ a lot.
+ sleeps
- sleep
- sleeping
- to sleep
E: "The cat" sama dengan "it", jadi sleep -> sleeps.

Q: Which sentence is correct?
+ He brushes his teeth twice a day.
- He brush his teeth twice a day.
- He brushing his teeth twice a day.
- He to brush his teeth twice a day.
E: Subjek "he" + kata kerja berakhiran -sh ditambah -es: brush -> brushes.
