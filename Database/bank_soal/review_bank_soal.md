## Level 2: Sympathy Expressions

### Skill 1: Expressing Sympathy (tingkat Dasar)

- A. Get well soon! ✅
- B. Congratulations!
- C. Happy birthday!
- D. Good job!

- B. See you later.
- C. What a beautiful day!
- D. I'm sorry to hear that. ✅

> Pembahasan: "I'm sorry to hear that" artinya "Aku turut sedih mendengarnya". Ini ungkapan simpati.

**4.** Your teacher has a toothache. You say, "...."

- B. Have a nice trip!
- C. Happy New Year!
- D. You're welcome.

> Pembahasan: "I hope you feel better soon" artinya "Semoga cepat merasa lebih baik", cocok untuk orang yang sakit.

- A. wins a game
- B. gets a present

- D. has a birthday

> Pembahasan: Ungkapan simpati dipakai saat orang lain mengalami hal yang menyedihkan atau kabar buruk.

## Level 3: Simple Present Tense

### Skill 5: Simple Present: Positive Sentences (tingkat Dasar)

**49.** She ____ milk every morning.

- A. drink
- B. drinks ✅
- C. drinking
- D. to drink

> Pembahasan: Subjek "she" memakai kata kerja + s/es: drink -> drinks.

**50.** I ____ my hands before eating.

- A. wash ✅
- B. washes
- C. washing
- D. to wash

> Pembahasan: Subjek "I" memakai kata kerja bentuk dasar tanpa -s: wash.

**51.** My father ____ to the office by car.

- A. go
- B. going
- C. goes ✅
- D. to go

> Pembahasan: "My father" sama dengan "he", jadi go -> goes (kata berakhiran -o ditambah -es).

**52.** They ____ football every Sunday.

- A. play ✅
- B. plays
- C. playing
- D. to play

> Pembahasan: Subjek "they" memakai kata kerja bentuk dasar: play.

**53.** The cat ____ a lot.

- A. sleep
- B. sleeping
- C. to sleep
- D. sleeps ✅

> Pembahasan: "The cat" sama dengan "it", jadi sleep -> sleeps.

**54.** Which sentence is correct?

- A. He brush his teeth twice a day.
- B. He brushes his teeth twice a day. ✅
- C. He brushing his teeth twice a day.
- D. He to brush his teeth twice a day.

> Pembahasan: Subjek "he" + kata kerja berakhiran -sh ditambah -es: brush -> brushes.
